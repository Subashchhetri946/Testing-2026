Here, I have made three unit tests for my rock-paper-scissor assignment.

In the 1st test, player chooses rock and computer choose paper. So, the computer wins.

In the 2nd test, player chooses paper and computer choose scissors. So, the computer wins.

In the 3rd test, player chooses rock and computer choose paper. So, the computer wins again.

In the 4th test, player chooses rock and computer choose scissors. So, the players wins now.

In the 5th test, player chooses scissors and computer choose paper. So, the players wins again.

In the end test, player chooses rock and computer choose rock. So, the result is draw.

These tests are enough for the basic requirements of this assignment because it checks all the three possible results : win, lose and draw. As you can see on the recording , i have done 6 tests to prove this point.
